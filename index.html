About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and a postgraduate research associate in Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London. At QMUL, I work in Ahmed M. Abdelmoniem's group.

My work spans the systems and networking stack for large-scale LLM training and serving. I have worked on RDMA-based network optimization, training frameworks, collective communication libraries, performance simulation, network fault detection, and cluster observability, as well as training and inference across heterogeneous GPUs and geo-distributed clusters.
